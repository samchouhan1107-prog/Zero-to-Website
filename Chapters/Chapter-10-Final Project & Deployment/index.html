Before going live

Production Checklist

Structure

Routes, files, components, assets, and imports are organized.

Experience

Navigation, interactions, forms, and responsive layouts work.

Quality

No broken assets, obvious console errors, or unfinished states.

Production

Build, hosting, HTTPS, domain, and live routes have been verified.
