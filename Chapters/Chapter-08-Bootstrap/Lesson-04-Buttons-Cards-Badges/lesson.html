1. The Anatomy of a Bootstrap Card

A .card is a flexible container with light border and clean border-radius. It encapsulates related information with distinct subcomponents.

  • .card-body: Main padded container
  • .card-title & .card-text: Typographic presets
  • .card-footer: Subtle bottom bar for actions and metadata
