Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

WebZoneBW SC ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website webzonebw.shop. This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, function more efficiently, and provide information to the website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for basic functions like user authentication, security, and session management.
  • Preference Cookies: Used to remember your choices such as UI theme (dark mode) or language preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our content and tools.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to serve relevant ads based on your visit to our and other websites.

3. Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to use certain features on our platform.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at privacy@webzonebw.shop.
