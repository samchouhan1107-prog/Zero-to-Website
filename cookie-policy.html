Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

WebZoneBW uses browser storage and, when you opt in, analytics cookies. This page explains the choices currently available.

Essential browser storage

The application uses browser storage for sign-in state, theme and interface preferences, local recovery copies of learning data, and your saved privacy choice. These items support features you request. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or remove local-only recovery copies; server-synced account data is handled separately.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is not loaded until you allow optional analytics in the Cookie settings panel. If enabled, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure use of the site. You can reject analytics or change a previous choice using Cookie settings in the application footer. Open the platform to manage your choice. The choice is stored in your browser.

Advertising cookies

Google AdSense is not enabled as of November 14, 2026, so this site is not currently setting AdSense advertising cookies. If ads are introduced, we will update this page and provide the consent interface required for the regions and advertising types served. Google requires publishers serving personalized ads in the EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland to use a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

Third-party services

When you use an optional provider-backed feature, such as analytics, AI assistance or payment checkout, the relevant provider may use its own cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. See the Privacy Policy for the providers and information categories involved.

Browser controls and publisher details

You can also manage or clear storage in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage can affect sign-in and saved preferences. See Contact & Publisher Details for official platform ownership information.
