Cookie and Local Storage Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

WebZoneBW SC uses browser storage to make the learning platform work and to remember a visitor’s chosen settings.

What is stored

  • Essential storage: supports signed-in learning progress and security.
  • Preference storage: remembers choices such as theme and reading settings.
  • Analytics: the site may use aggregated usage data to find broken or unclear parts of the learning experience. This page will be updated before a new analytics provider is introduced.

Managing storage

You can manage or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Removing them may reset saved preferences or affect signed-in features.
