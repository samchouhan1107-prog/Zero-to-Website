1. The Single Source of Truth Pattern

Rather than scraping data directly from HTML tags, maintain your state in JavaScript data structures (arrays and objects). When an action occurs, update the state, save to localStorage, and re-render the view.

  • State: let tasks = JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('tasks') || '[]');
  • Update: tasks.push(newTask);
  • Persist & Render: saveTasks(); renderUI();
