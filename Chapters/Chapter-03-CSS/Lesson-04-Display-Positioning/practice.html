Practice Challenge 3.4

Build an Absolute Notification Badge on a Card

Use position: relative on the parent card and position: absolute with top and right offsets to pin a notification pill badge to the top corner.

🎯 Challenge Tasks

  • 1. Ensure the parent card has position: relative; so the badge positions relative to it.
  • 2. Give the badge position: absolute; top: 12px; right: 12px;.
  • 3. Add display: inline-block; and padding to the card action button.
