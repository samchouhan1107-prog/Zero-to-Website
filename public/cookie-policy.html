Cookie & Advertising Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This Cookie & Advertising Policy explains how WebZoneBW SC uses cookies and similar technologies, and how Google AdSense advertising operates on our platform. This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners. Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain until they expire or are deleted).

3. Third-Party Cookies

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and usage patterns. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about how visitors use our site, including the number of visitors, the pages they visit, and how they arrived at our site. This information is anonymized and used only to improve our platform. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our platform. AdSense uses cookies to:

  • Serve personalized ads: Based on your browsing history and interests (via the DoubleClick cookie)
  • Limit ad frequency: Control how many times you see a particular ad
  • Measure ad effectiveness: Track ad impressions, clicks, and conversions
  • Prevent fraud: Detect and prevent invalid clicks and impressions

Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by using the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Even if you opt out of personalized advertising, you may still see ads. These ads will be non-personalized and based on the content of the page you are viewing rather than your interests.

Auth0

Our authentication system uses Auth0, which may place essential cookies to maintain your login session and ensure secure authentication. These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options for managing cookies:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Check your browser's help section for instructions.
  • Our Cookie Banner: When you first visit our site, you can accept all cookies or customize your preferences through our consent banner.
  • Google Ad Controls: Visit Google Ads Settings to manage ad personalization.
  • Network Advertising Initiative: Use the NAI opt-out page to control ads from multiple networks.

5. Ad Content and Standards

All advertisements displayed on WebZoneBW SC are served through Google AdSense and are subject to Google's content policies. We do not directly control the specific ads shown, but we actively monitor for ads that may be:

  • Misleading or deceptive
  • Containing malware or phishing attempts
  • Inappropriate for our educational audience
  • Violating Google's advertising policies

If you encounter any problematic ads, please report them to ads@webzonebw.shop.

6. GDPR & ePrivacy Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we comply with the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR requirements regarding cookies. We obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising) on your device. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or contacting us.

Our consent management system implements the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) where applicable to ensure compliant ad serving for European users.

7. Do Not Track Signals

We respect Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals. When a DNT signal is detected, we disable Google Analytics tracking and non-essential advertising cookies. Essential cookies required for the Service to function will still be active.
