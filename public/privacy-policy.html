Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

WebZoneBW SC operates webzonebw.shop. This page explains the information involved when you use the learning platform.

Information used by the platform

If you create an account or save learning progress, the platform uses the details needed to provide that feature, such as account information and lesson-progress data. Browser storage may also remember preferences such as theme and reading settings.

How it is used

Information is used to operate requested features, keep the platform secure, respond to support messages, and improve a reported problem. WebZoneBW SC does not currently serve advertising on the site.

Third-party processing

The optional AI tutor may send the prompt you submit to its AI service provider to generate a response. Do not submit passwords, private keys, or sensitive personal information to the tutor or a practice editor.

Your choices and contact

You can clear browser storage in your browser settings. To ask about information associated with an account or a support request, contact contact@webzonebw.in.

Updates

We will update this page before a material change to these practices takes effect.
