Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Our commitment

Zero-to-Website is published under the WebZoneBW identity by Sameer Chouhan. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and shared when you use the educational platform.

2. Information we collect

  • Accounts and sessions: name, email address, sign-in method and session information. Passwords submitted for account sign-in are handled by the authentication service; do not send passwords through support messages.
  • Learning and user content: courses, lessons, progress, quiz and practice results, bookmarks, notes, saved snippets, lesson submissions, drafts, code examples and contributions you choose to save or publish. Some features keep a browser copy for recovery and synchronize with the platform server when available.
  • Purchases: subscription, licence, order and payment-status records when you use a paid feature. The payment provider enabled for a transaction may be Stripe or Razorpay. Payment providers handle payment processing under their own terms and privacy notices.
  • AI features: when you choose an AI tutor or generation feature, the prompt and relevant lesson context you submit are sent to Google's Gemini service through the platform server to produce a response.
  • Analytics and preferences: Google Analytics is optional. It is loaded only after you allow analytics in Cookie settings. Your analytics choice is stored in your browser. Essential sign-in, theme and feature state may also use browser storage.
  • Technical data: browser and device details, IP address, and site activity may be available to the platform and its hosting or delivery providers for request handling, security and troubleshooting. Google Analytics receives usage data only if you allow analytics.
  • Technical delivery: requests pass through the hosting and delivery services used to operate the site, currently including Render and Cloudflare. Google Fonts may receive a request from your browser when the site loads its fonts.

3. How we use information

We use these information categories to create and secure accounts, provide lessons and saved learning features, respond to requests, operate subscriptions and payments, generate AI responses when requested, maintain the site, and understand usage when analytics is enabled.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The platform uses essential browser storage for sign-in state, preferences and local recovery. Optional Google Analytics stays off unless you allow it through Cookie settings. As of this update, Google AdSense is not enabled on the public site. If advertising is introduced, this policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated before ads are served. The site will use a Google-certified consent management platform where required for the regions and ad types served, including applicable requirements for the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland.

5. AI and third-party services

When you use an AI tutor or generation feature, the prompt and relevant lesson context you submit are sent to Google's Gemini service through the platform server. Analytics, payment, hosting, security and font providers may process information needed for their services under their own terms and privacy practices. If you use an external website linked from this platform, its own privacy policy applies. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the platform and its users.

6. Data protection and retention

We apply safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration and disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Server-side account, learning, Storehouse and transaction records are retained as needed to provide the platform and meet operational, security or legal obligations. Browser storage may also hold preferences, sign-in state and local recovery copies.

7. Your privacy choices

You can accept or reject optional analytics and change your saved choice using Cookie settings in the application footer. Depending on applicable law, you may also request access to, correction of or deletion of eligible personal information, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

8. Children's privacy

The platform is educational. Where children use our services, applicable age restrictions, parental consent requirements and child-protection laws must be followed. A parent or guardian who has a privacy concern may contact us.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes to platform features, technology or legal requirements. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
